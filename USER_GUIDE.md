# NEXUS OS User Guide

Welcome to NEXUS OS! This guide walks you through using the desktop environment and its built-in suite of tools.

---

## 1. Desktop & Window Management

- **Dragging Windows**: Click and hold any window's title bar to drag it across the desktop.
- **Resizing**: Hover over any of the 8 edges or corners of a window until the resize cursor appears, then click and drag to resize.
- **Snapping**: Drag a window title bar to the top edge to maximize. Drag to the left or right edges of the screen to snap to a half-screen layout.
- **Double-Click Title Bar**: Quickly toggles between maximized and restored states.
- **Alt + Tab**: Hold `Alt` and press `Tab` to cycle through open applications.
- **Show Desktop**: Click the small sliver at the far right of the taskbar to minimize all windows.

---

## 2. Drag-and-Drop File Import

1. Open **Nexus Explorer** or look at the Nexus Desktop canvas.
2. Select any file or folder in your real Windows File Explorer or on your Windows Desktop.
3. Drag it over the Nexus OS window and release the mouse.
4. Nexus OS will copy the item into your workspace (`Desktop` or current folder) and display a notification.

---

## 3. Web Browsing & Downloads

- Launch **Nexus Navigator** from the desktop or Start Menu.
- Enter any URL or search query in the address bar.
- When downloading a `.exe` installer:
  1. Open the **Downloads** panel on the right.
  2. Click **Inspect Security** to view its Authenticode digital signature, publisher, and SHA-256 hash.
  3. Click **Run on Host...** to launch the installer on Windows after confirming the security prompt.

---

## 4. Personalization & Settings

- Launch **Nexus Settings**:
  - **Themes**: Switch between Dark and Light mode.
  - **Accent Colors**: Choose from Royal Blue, Cyber Purple, Emerald, Crimson, Amber, or Cyan.
  - **Wallpapers**: Choose curated presets (Nexus Aurora, Cyber Horizon, Nordic Dark, Sunset Dunes) or browse your computer for a custom image.
  - **Window Style**: Adjust window corner radius slider and transparency.
  - **Reduced Motion**: Enable for instant window transitions on lower-end PCs.

---

## 5. Built-in Games & Utilities

- **Nexus Arcade**:
  - **Minesweeper**: Click cells to reveal, right-click to place flags.
  - **Cyber Snake**: Use arrow keys or WASD to navigate and collect food.
  - **2D Cyber Runner**: Press Spacebar or Up Arrow to jump over obstacles.
  - **Sudoku**: Select a cell and choose digits 1-9 to solve the puzzle.
- **Nexus Terminal**:
  - Built-in commands: `help`, `sysinfo`, `ls`, `cd`, `cat`, `mkdir`, `rm`, `ps`.
  - Host execution: Run `host <command>` (e.g. `host dir`) to safely run a command on host Windows PowerShell with confirmation.
- **Widgets & Rainmeter**:
  - View real-time CPU and RAM utilization.
  - Write sticky notes that persist during your session.
  - Access the Rainmeter compatibility bridge.
